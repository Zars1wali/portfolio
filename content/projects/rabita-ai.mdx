## What Rabita AI Is

Rabita AI is a production-grade, WhatsApp-native **dual-agent AI commerce platform** built to autonomously run sales operations for Pakistani retail businesses — entirely inside WhatsApp, backed by an enterprise **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Responsible AI framework**.

It is not a chatbot. It is a **deployed AI sales and inventory system** that handles real customer inquiries, delivers verified product photos with live pricing, processes voice notes in Roman Urdu, and lets the shop owner manage prices and stock through casual WhatsApp messages — no admin dashboard, no web portal.

**First production deployment:** Haider Arms, a licensed firearms dealership in Pakistan. Running 24/7 on a Vultr Cloud VPS since August 2026.

## Responsible AI: Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Architecture

In regulated commercial domains like firearms and defense retail in Pakistan, deploying autonomous AI requires strict safety guardrails. Rabta AI is architected from the ground up as a **Responsible AI System**, establishing a clear boundary between automated catalog intelligence and binding commercial decisions.

### Why Human-in-the-Loop is Mandatory

Autonomous language models must never make unilateral commitments regarding firearms shipping or regulatory compliance. In real-world Pakistani arms retail, three human factors govern every transaction:

1. **Legal Talks & Licensing Formalities:**
   - Buying firearms requires verified National Identity Cards (CNIC), valid provincial/federal licenses, and adherence to legal quotas.
   - Government police No-Objection Certificates (NOCs) and district restrictions can vary significantly between provinces.
   - The AI answers factual catalog specs and pricing, but **hands off legal eligibility reviews directly to the licensed store owner**.

2. **Delivery Protocols & Transit Safety:**
   - Regulated weapons and ammunition cannot be shipped via standard third-party couriers (such as TCS or Leopards).
   - Delivery depends heavily on **nature of the item**, road transit safety, police checkpoints, and authorized dealer escort.
   - The AI never quotes automated shipping guarantees; it flags out-of-city delivery requests as priority human-escalation items.

3. **Volatile Stock Availability & Human In-Store Factors:**
   - Physical brick-and-mortar customers frequently walk in and purchase firearms with cash, changing SKU availability within minutes.
   - Human judgment is needed to allocate limited high-demand inventory (e.g. Austrian Glock 19s vs. USA variants) before committing to WhatsApp buyers.

### How the Responsible AI Loop Operates

```text
[Customer Ingress] ──▶ Customer asks: "Rawalpindi delivery possible hai aur license verification?"
                            │
                            ▼
[ReACT Agent] ────────▶ Detects delivery & licensing intent → Calls escalate_inquiry tool
                            │
                            ▼
[Ticket Created] ─────▶ PostgreSQL logs #ESC-XX with customer context & phone number
                            │
                            ▼
[WhatsApp Alert] ─────▶ Instant priority alert sent to Store Owner (Haider) via WhatsApp
                            │
                            ▼
[2-Hour Mute Guard] ──▶ AI automatically freezes response buffers for that customer thread
                            │
                            ▼
[Human Resolution] ───▶ Store owner takes over directly, ensures 100% legal compliance
```

- **Tool Trigger (`escalate_inquiry`):** When a sensitive inquiry occurs, the customer sales agent dispatches an escalation ticket instead of attempting to improvise answers.
- **2-Hour Takeover Guard:** The moment the store owner reviews the ticket and replies to the customer thread, the edge gateway detects the owner's phone ID and mutes all AI replies for that thread for 2 hours, preventing embarrassing AI/human collision.
- **Accountability Guarantee:** Every legal talk, custom quote, and delivery arrangement is handled with authoritative human accountability.

## Layer 2 — Edge Gateway (Meta Official WhatsApp Business Platform)

The WhatsApp gateway connects to the **Meta Official WhatsApp Business Platform** via Cloud API webhooks, replacing brittle WebSocket scrapers with enterprise-grade infrastructure.

### 15-Second Sliding Burst Debounce

Pakistani customers send messages in rapid bursts — 4 to 6 short messages in a row instead of one composed message. Without debouncing, the AI would generate a separate reply for each fragment.

- **How it works:** When a customer sends a message, a 15-second timer starts. Every subsequent message resets the timer. Only when the customer pauses for a full 15 seconds does the gateway aggregate all messages into a single payload and forward it to the backend.
- **Typing detection integration:** If the gateway detects the customer is still typing (via WhatsApp `presence.update` → `composing`), it extends the timer automatically — preventing premature AI responses.
- **Owner fast lane:** The store owner's messages use a 1.5-second debounce instead, since owner commands are typically single administrative instructions.

### 2-Hour Owner Takeover Guard

When the store owner manually replies to a customer on WhatsApp, the AI must immediately stand down.

- **Trigger:** Owner sends a manual message to a customer thread.
- **Action:** AI is auto-muted for that specific customer for 2 hours. All pending AI response buffers for that customer are cancelled.
- **Purpose:** Prevents AI/human collision where both the owner and the AI reply simultaneously.

### Contact & Privacy ID Resolution

WhatsApp assigns privacy identifiers for user messaging. The gateway maintains a persistent resolver that correlates incoming contact payloads with verified customer CRM records and escalation ticket tracking.

## Layer 3 — Dual-Agent Engine (FastAPI & LangGraph)

The backend runs two separate ReACT agents with different configurations, each optimized for its role.

### Customer Sales Agent (Temperature: 0.5)

A warm, conversational sales persona that speaks natural Pakistani Roman Urdu. Designed to build trust and close deals.

- **Persona:** Friendly, respectful, culturally aware — uses greetings like *"Jee bhai"* (English: "Yes, brother") and *"Bilkul"* (English: "Absolutely")
- **Response discipline:** 1 to 3 sentences maximum. Never sends walls of text.
- **Sales-aggressive:** Proactively guides undecided customers toward purchase decisions by asking about budget, use case, and preferences.
- **Order intake funnel:** When a customer confirms interest, the agent collects: (1) Name, (2) City, (3) Delivery address, (4) SIM number — in that sequence.

### Owner Co-Pilot Agent (Temperature: 0.2)

A strict, deterministic engine for business operations. Low temperature ensures precise, predictable execution.

- **Catalog mutations:** Processes natural language commands like *"Glock 19 price 390k kardo"* (English: "Set the Glock 19 price to 390,000") and executes exact SQL updates.
- **Inventory control:** Toggles stock status via casual messages like *"Tisas out of stock"* (English: "Mark Tisas as out of stock").
- **Two-way escalation relay:** When a customer escalation requires the owner's input, the owner's reply is intercepted and relayed back to the waiting customer thread automatically.

## Layer 4 — ReACT Tool Calling

Every tool call is grounded against the live PostgreSQL database. The AI never fabricates product specs, prices, or availability.

### Customer Tools

**`search_catalog`** — Queries the product database with zero-hallucination guarantees. Uses exact SQL matching for brands, models, and calibers. Decimal calibers like `5.56`, `.308`, and `7.62` are preserved as atomic values — never split by tokenizers.

**`get_product_photos`** — Retrieves verified studio photographs from the static media server. Each photo is served via Caddy with a dynamic caption showing the product name and current price.

**`check_delivery_policy`** — Returns the dealership's delivery terms: managed shipping, 100% advance payment required, and city-specific availability.

**`get_payment_bank_details`** — Provides the dealership's verified bank account details for advance payments.

**`escalate_inquiry`** — Creates a structured ESC-XX ticket when a customer question requires the owner's input (delivery logistics, legal licensing talks, bulk orders, custom pricing). The ticket is forwarded to the owner's WhatsApp with all customer context.

### Owner Tools

**`update_product_price`** — Executes a direct SQL price update on the product catalog. Returns confirmation with old price vs. new price.

**`toggle_in_stock`** — Flips a product's availability status between in-stock and out-of-stock.

## Layer 5 — Foundation Model: Google Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite

**Google Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite** with native ReACT function calling.

- **Why Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite:** Sub-second inference latency critical for WhatsApp real-time commerce. Native function calling eliminates brittle regex parsers. Exceptional comprehension of code-switched Roman Urdu and English without fine-tuning.
- **Cost & Token Efficiency:** Delivers first tokens in &lt;450ms, enabling complete ReACT reasoning loops in under 850ms on WhatsApp.
- **Precision:** Low temperature execution guarantees deterministic tool selection without conversational drift.

## Layer 6 — Data Layer & Persistence

### PostgreSQL 16 + pgvector

Single source of truth for the entire platform. Stores:
- **Tenant configurations** — multi-tenant architecture supporting different business clients
- **Product catalog** — every SKU with brand, model, caliber, price (PKR), origin country, and stock status
- **Escalation tickets** — ESC-XX records with customer context, owner responses, and resolution timestamps
- **2-hour mute records** — tracks which customer threads have active owner takeovers
- **AI persona configurations** — system prompts, temperature settings, and behavioral rules per tenant

### Static Media Server

Product photographs are stored on disk at `/static/catalog_images/` and served through Caddy's static file server with automatic HTTPS. Photos are verified against product records — the AI can only deliver images that exist in the catalog.

## Voice Intelligence — Deepgram Nova-3

Pakistani customers heavily prefer sending voice notes over typing. Deepgram Nova-3 handles bilingual speech-to-text:

1. Customer sends a `.ogg` voice note on WhatsApp
2. Gateway detects audio media and forwards the binary to the backend
3. Backend streams the audio to Deepgram's Nova-3 STT API
4. Deepgram transcribes code-switched Roman Urdu and English into structured text (under 400ms)
5. Transcribed text enters the same LangGraph pipeline as typed messages

**Example:** A customer sends a 4-second voice note: *"Salam bhai, Glock ki price kya hai?"* (English: "Hello brother, what's the Glock's price?"). Deepgram Nova-3 transcribes in 320ms. The sales agent responds with exact pricing within 1 second.

## Pilot Client — Haider Arms

Haider Arms is a legally licensed firearms dealership in Pakistan. This domain was chosen as the first deployment because it is one of the most unforgiving environments for AI:

- **Zero tolerance for hallucination.** Quoting wrong calibers (confusing 9mm with .45 ACP, or 5.56 with 7.62) or fabricating non-existent products would destroy credibility instantly.
- **Legal compliance & Responsible AI.** The AI strictly quotes from the verified catalog and steers customers toward licensed shop visits, escalating any delivery or legal licensing talks directly to the human owner.
- **Exact pricing.** High-value inventory requires PKR-accurate quotes with clear distinctions between import variants (e.g., Glock USA vs. Glock Austria).

## The Engineering Story

### The Over-Engineering Failure

Early iterations used an 8-node LangGraph with regex pre-parsers, an LLM critic node, and multi-model fallback chains. In live testing with Haider Arms customers, this architecture failed catastrophically:

- Regex pre-parsers stripped customer messages like *"yes show me that"* (English: "yes, show me that one") down to just *"yes"* — causing the AI to hallucinate a product named "Yes."
- Caliber strings like `5.56` were split into `5` and `56` by tokenizers, penalizing genuine rifles.
- The critic node added double LLM round-trips, inflating response times to 15 seconds on WhatsApp.

### The Breakthrough

Sitting with the shop owner and watching live customer conversations made the answer clear: real WhatsApp users do not follow state diagrams. They code-switch, use slang, send voice notes, and fire 5 messages in 2 seconds.

The fix: strip everything back to the essentials.

- **3 clean nodes** replaced the 8-node graph (router, customer agent, owner agent)
- **Zero regex surgery** — let Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite's native reasoning handle raw customer input
- **15-second debounce** — aggregate burst messages into coherent context before invoking AI
- **Human-in-the-Loop guard** — prevent AI/human collision when the owner steps in for legal/delivery talks

Result: sub-second response times, zero hallucinations, and a system that Haider Arms customers interact with as naturally as texting a real salesperson.
