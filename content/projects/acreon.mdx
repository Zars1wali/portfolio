## What Acreon Is

Acreon is an enterprise **B2B2X satellite supply-chain risk platform** engineered for global food conglomerates, agri-processors, and commodity buyers (such as rice mills, sugar refineries, and multinational brands like Nestlé and Matco Foods).

It is fundamentally **not** a farmer-facing app, nor is it a generic NDVI gadget. It solves the enterprise procurement team's greatest operational nightmare: **supply shocks discovered too late**. 

Traditionally, buyers learn that their contracted suppliers suffered severe crop loss (from drought, waterlogging, or disease) on the day of harvest — when it is too late to prevent contract defaults, factory downtime, or price spikes. Acreon transforms free, publicly accessible **European Space Agency (ESA) Sentinel-2 multispectral satellite imagery** into a **3 to 8 week early-warning advantage**, giving buyers the lead time needed to re-hedge procurement, dispatch field scouts, or reroute supply chains.

## The "Acreon Firewall": Observation Over Speculation

The agricultural technology sector is crowded with tools making unverified claims — promising automated yield guarantees, prescription fertilizer recommendations, or remote pest diagnoses purely from orbit. 

Acreon operates under a foundational architectural and legal principle known as the **Acreon Firewall**:

> **"Acreon reports relative vigor anomalies and scouting priorities — never automated agronomic prescriptions."**

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       THE ACREON FIREWALL                   │
├──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┤
│  ✅ ACREON OUTPUTS           │  ❌ ACREON PROHIBITS         │
├──────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────┤
│ • "Vigor in Zone C dropped   │ • "Apply 45 kg N/ha here."   │
│   18% vs. previous pass."    │   (Requires soil chemistry)  │
│ • "Biomass tracking 3 weeks  │ • "Fungal leaf blight found."│
│   behind 5-yr baseline."     │   (Requires pathology test)  │
│ • "High risk across 14 paddy │ • "Guaranteed harvest will   │
│   sourcing fields."          │   reach 4.2 t/ha."           │
└──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┘
```

### Why This Boundary is Absolute:
1. **Agronomic Liability:** Prescribing chemicals or fertilizer without ground soil tests, growth stage calibration, and licensed agronomists exposes platforms to product-label violations and legal liability.
2. **Honesty by Construction:** A satellite pixel is a 10×10 meter reflection of electromagnetic light. An anomaly means: *"something is stressing this sector — go inspect it"* — never a manufactured diagnosis.
3. **Mandatory Cloud Masking:** Unmasked satellite math silently corrupts numbers. Acreon enforces strict scene classification masking (SCL + `s2cloudless`) before computing any vegetation indices. Every reading ships with its explicit **capture date** and **valid-pixel percentage**.

## The Tech Stack

### Frontend & Cartographic Interface
* **Next.js 14 (App Router) & TypeScript:** Enterprise dashboard delivering server-side rendered portfolio summaries, strict TypeScript interfaces, and responsive layout primitives.
* **MapLibre GL JS & terra-draw:** WebGL-accelerated map engine providing vector polygon drawing and editing for Areas of Interest (AOIs). Avoids commercial token lock-in (e.g., Mapbox) while delivering 60 FPS raster tile rendering.
* **Tailwind CSS:** Custom dark-mode luxury enterprise design tokens with high-contrast cartographic visualization (`#00E5FF` cyan vector highlights).

### Backend & Geospatial Core
* **FastAPI (Python 3.12) & Pydantic v2:** Asynchronous REST API layer enforcing strict input validation, multi-tenant organization isolation (`org_id` scoped across all database queries), and role-based access control.
* **PostgreSQL 16 & PostGIS:** Spatial database storing field geometries as `geometry(Polygon, 4326)`, spatial indexing (`GIST`), multi-field bounding queries, and polygonized management zone boundaries.
* **pystac-client:** SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog (STAC) client executing spatial queries against AWS Earth Search and Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem (CDSE) to discover recent low-cloud Sentinel-2 scenes.
* **rioxarray & stackstac:** Lazy, windowed Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF (COG) byte-range reads. Bounded strictly to field coordinates, eliminating the need to download 500MB+ full satellite granules.
* **scikit-learn & numpy:** Unsupervised spatial clustering (`k-means`, k=3) executing raster-to-vector polygonization for automated field management zone generation.

### Tiling & Asynchronous Infrastructure
* **TiTiler (`rio-tiler`):** Dynamic tile server reading processed index COGs directly from cloud storage, serving XYZ raster tiles with on-the-fly colormaps and dynamic dynamic stretch rescaling.
* **arq + Redis:** Asynchronous background job queue running long-running STAC downloads, cloud-masking calculations, and automated daily alert scans (`scan_for_alerts()`).
* **Cloudflare R2 / AWS S3:** High-throughput object storage housing generated Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs (COGs) and analytical exports.
* **Docker Compose:** Containerized orchestration ensuring parity between local development and cloud production deployments.

## The Geospatial Processing Pipeline

Band math is straightforward; **pipeline correctness is where real-world systems succeed or fail**. Acreon's worker executes a deterministic 7-step pipeline for every field analysis:

```text
STAC Search ──► Windowed Read ──► Cloud Mask (SCL) ──► Index Math ──► Stats ──► k-means Zones ──► Dynamic COG
 (<20% cloud)    (B04,B05,B08,SCL)  (s2cloudless cross)   (NDVI / NDRE)   (p10..p90)    (k=3 vector)     (TiTiler)
```

### 1. Scene Discovery & Selection (`geo/stac.py`)
Queries STAC endpoints over the field's bounding box for candidate Sentinel-2 Level-2A (surface reflectance) scenes over a rolling 30-day window. Automatically filters and selects the scene with the lowest cloud cover under 20% (or widens to 90 days during persistent monsoon events).

### 2. Windowed COG Reads (`geo/read.py`)
Rather than downloading full 100km×100km satellite granules, the worker performs windowed HTTP range reads targeting only the exact geographic bounding box of the field for the required spectral bands:
* **B04 (Red — 665 nm)**
* **B05 (Red Edge 1 — 705 nm)**
* **B08 (Near-Infrared / NIR — 842 nm)**
* **SCL (Scene Classification Layer — 20m)**

*Multi-tile UTM coordinate reference system (CRS) reprojections are handled explicitly to eliminate boundary seam artifacts.*

### 3. Cloud Masking & Validity Verification (`geo/mask.py`)
The pipeline filters out corrupted pixels using the Scene Classification Layer (SCL), purging:
* Cloud shadows (Class 3)
* Medium/high cloud probability (Classes 8 & 9)
* Thin cirrus (Class 10)
* Snow/ice (Class 11)

Masked arrays are cross-checked with `s2cloudless` cloud probability masks. A **validity percentage** (`valid_pixels / total_aoi_pixels`) is computed. If validity drops below 15%, the analysis is flagged as `low_validity` and management zones are suppressed to prevent misleading the buyer.

### 4. Spectral Index Derivation (`services/indices.py`)
Computed exclusively over valid, non-masked pixels:
* **NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index):**
  - Formula: `(B08_NIR - B04_Red) / (B08_NIR + B04_Red)`
  - *General biomass, leaf area index, and cellular plant vigor.*
* **NDRE (Normalized Difference Red Edge):**
  - Formula: `(B08_NIR - B05_RedEdge) / (B08_NIR + B05_RedEdge)`
  - *Chlorophyll absorption proxy; penetrates deep into dense, closed crop canopies (e.g., mature rice/maize) where standard NDVI saturates.*

### 5. Statistical Telemetry
Calculates mean, median, min, max, 10th percentile (p10), 90th percentile (p90), and valid pixel count across the field.

### 6. Management Zone Clustering (`services/zones.py`)
Applies k-means clustering (k = 3) over the masked raster to segment the field into three distinct operational performance zones:
* **Zone 1 (Underperforming / Stressed):** Priority sector requiring immediate physical scouting.
* **Zone 2 (Moderate Baseline):** Standard canopy performance.
* **Zone 3 (Vigorous Canopy):** High biomass / optimal cellular vigor.

The clusters are vectorized into GeoJSON polygons, reprojected, and stored in PostGIS.

### 7. Tiling & Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF Generation (`geo/tiling.py`)
Writes the final floating-point index array as a Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF (COG) with internal overview pyramids. TiTiler serves these as XYZ raster tiles (`/tiles/<analysis_id>/<z>/<x>/<y>.png`) directly into MapLibre GL JS with dynamic color ramp shaders.

## Key Platform Features

* **Portfolio-Level Risk Oversight:** Buyers monitor hundreds of supplier fields simultaneously from a unified screen, triaged automatically by relative vigor drops rather than inspecting individual fields in isolation.
* **Interactive Field Boundary Onboarding:** Field managers can draw custom polygonal AOIs with `terra-draw` or upload standard GeoJSON/KML files, instantly initiating automated historical scene backfills.
* **Multi-Layer Cartographic Switcher:** Real-time toggling between Sentinel-2 True Color, NDVI Relative Vigor, NDRE Chlorophyll, and Vectorized Management Zones.
* **Multi-Pass Vigor Trajectory Tracking:** Compares current vegetation performance against regional historical baselines across the crop cycle to detect subtle anomalies weeks before harvest.
* **Ground-Truth Calibration Loop:** Designed to integrate with field scouting reports and companion **IRIS IoT soil sensor hardware** to calibrate satellite anomaly detections over time.
