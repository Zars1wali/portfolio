Acreon monitors supplier fields from space, giving procurement teams early
warning on crop stress, flooding, and vigor decline before it shows up in
harvest tonnage. It processes Sentinel-2 spectral bands for NDVI/NDRE vigor
tracking, cross-references radar (SAR) for flood mapping that works through
cloud cover, and closes the loop with field scouts who verify flagged
anomalies on the ground — their observations recalibrate the model.

The product's core positioning is what we call the "Acreon Firewall": we
report risk signals and lead-time alerts, and explicitly refuse to issue
agronomic prescriptions (fertilizer rates, pest diagnoses, yield guarantees)
that competitors promise but can't actually justify from orbit. Every
analytic ships with its capture date and valid-pixel percentage, so buyers
know exactly how much to trust a given read.

Ground-truth is added by IRIS, our companion IoT sensor platform. Currently
in active B2B outreach across Pakistan, Bangladesh, the UAE/Gulf, and
Europe, alongside applications to agtech accelerators (QSTP, Hub71, Startup
Qatar) for a Qatar market entry.
