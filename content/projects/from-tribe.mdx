## What From Tribe Is

From Tribe is a **live, production multi-vendor marketplace** for independent EU artisan and craft brands. It targets a real gap: small and medium creative brands (fashion, accessories, home, lifestyle) who are priced out of visibility on Amazon and Etsy by ad auction dynamics.

The model: **13% commission per transaction, zero listing fees, zero forced discounting, zero ad spend required**. Discovery is organic — quality and editorial curation, not budget. The company is registered as **Fromtribe OÜ** in Tallinn, Estonia, with EU statutory consumer protection (14-day returns), DPD & InPost tracked delivery, and multi-currency (EUR primary).

**The site is live at [fromtribe.com](https://www.fromtribe.com)** — Next.js 14 App Router confirmed from production HTML headers and chunk manifests.

## The Core Architecture Decision: Why Medusa.js Over Shopify

This was the biggest technical decision of the project, and it was made in **Phase 3** after evaluating three options:

| Platform | What It Offers | Why We Rejected It |
|---|---|---|
| **Shopify** | Mature ecosystem, low setup overhead | $0.15–0.30 per transaction fee on top of Stripe cuts margin. No true multi-vendor native support — requires expensive third-party apps. Zero control over checkout UX and data. |
| **WooCommerce** | WordPress ecosystem, cheap | PHP stack — incompatible with our Next.js TypeScript architecture. Monolithic — no headless support for custom storefront. |
| **Medusa.js** ✅ | Open-source headless commerce engine | Full control over data, checkout, and payout logic. Multi-vendor order routing is natively extensible. Node.js — aligns with our TypeScript stack. No per-transaction platform fees. |

**Nuno's architecture guidance:** Medusa's plugin system allowed us to implement custom vendor payout splitting without rebuilding Stripe Connect from scratch. The headless pattern meant our Next.js frontend could have full editorial control — dynamic ISR for product pages, React Server Components for performance.

## Vendor Payout Architecture (Stripe Connect)

This was the most technically complex piece. When a customer buys from multiple vendors in one cart:

1. **Single Stripe PaymentIntent** captures the full cart amount.
2. Medusa's order routing splits the order into **per-vendor sub-orders**.
3. Each vendor's Stripe Connect account receives their portion minus the **13% platform commission**, automatically routed via Stripe Transfer API.
4. The platform's Stripe account retains the commission.
5. **SendGrid** fires payout confirmation emails to each vendor with their per-order breakdown.

This eliminated the need for any manual commission accounting — every payout is automatic, auditable, and reconcilable from the Stripe dashboard.
